# Business and Product Roadmap

Status: Needs validation
Confidence: Medium
Source: PRODUCT_BLUEPRINT.md, ARCHITECTURE.md

## Canonical roadmap view
1. Establish a coherent product experience around discovery, chat, and booking.
2. Validate trust/escrow and verification flows.
3. Expand the platform into broader ecosystem capabilities such as learning, referrals, and intelligence.
4. Harden the platform for production with real services, operations, and compliance.

## Current evidence
- The repository already contains modules for all of the above areas.
- However, the current implementation should be treated as an integrated prototype and not a completed production roadmap.
- The sequence above is an inferred direction from existing docs and code, not a validated launch plan.
