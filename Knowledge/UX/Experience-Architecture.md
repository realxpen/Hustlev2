# Experience Architecture

Status: Current
Confidence: High
Source: PRODUCT_BLUEPRINT.md, src/components/MockHome.tsx

## Canonical summary
The user journey is organized around a discovery-to-transaction loop. Users begin in a social feed, move into profile/chat contexts, then into booking, payment, and reputation flows.

## Main experience layers
- Discovery: feed and nearby discovery.
- Intent: search and profile exploration.
- Connection: chat and activity.
- Transaction: booking, wallet, and escrow.
- Identity evolution: profile mode transition and trust/review loops.

## Current implementation evidence
- The home surface contains feed, discovery, chat, bookings, wallet, trust, and creator experiences.

## Interpretation
- The architecture is coherent and strongly aligned with the product blueprint.
