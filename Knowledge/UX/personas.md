## Personas implied by the repository
- The curious client: discovers local talent and wants low-friction service access.
- The emerging hustler: wants to make their skills visible and monetize them.
- The trust-sensitive buyer/seller: needs safety, reassurance, and clear transaction outcomes.

## Evidence
- The product blueprint describes client-to-hustler evolution and trust-related friction.

## Gap
