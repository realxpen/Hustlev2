# UX Principles

Status: Current
Confidence: High
Source: PRODUCT_BLUEPRINT.md

## Principles captured from the repository
- Make the experience feel kinetic, social, and local.
- Keep friction low for initial participation.
- Make trust visible and emotionally reassuring.
- Treat transactions as extensions of conversation, not a separate process.
- Let identity evolve through action and feedback.

## Current implementation evidence
- The app includes animated transitions, a unified home experience, and system-level overlays for chat, payments, trust, and activity.
