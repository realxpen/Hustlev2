# Product Principles

Status: Current
Confidence: High
Source: PRODUCT_BLUEPRINT.md

## Principles captured from the repository
- Social first, economic second.
- Identity as currency.
- Local-first relevance.
- Trust through transparency.
- Curiosity over search.
- Achievement-driven identity evolution.

## How these principles appear in the implementation
- The feed is central and immersive.
- Profiles contain social and economic signals.
- Chat and bookings are linked rather than separated.
- Trust and escrow are built into the flow.

## Implementation note
- These principles are visible in the current UI and architecture, but the product experience remains a prototype rather than a final, validated product design.
