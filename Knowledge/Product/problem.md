# Problem Space

Status: Current
Confidence: High
Source: PRODUCT_BLUEPRINT.md

## Canonical summary
Hustle addresses a market problem: informal and local service economies are fragmented, trust is hard to establish, and transactions often happen outside structured systems. The platform attempts to reduce friction between discovering talent, evaluating trust, negotiating work, and paying safely.

## Core pain points
- People struggle to discover nearby skilled providers in a social, low-friction way.
- Payment and service trust are often unclear or risky in informal transactions.
- Individuals who do work outside formal employment lack a clear digital identity and feedback loop.

## Evidence from the repository
- The product blueprint explicitly discusses escrow, trust, and local discovery as central needs.
- The implementation contains booking, review, escrow, trust, and verification features that map directly to these pain points.
