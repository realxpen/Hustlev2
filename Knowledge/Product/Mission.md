# Hustle Mission

Status: Current
Confidence: High
Source: PRODUCT_BLUEPRINT.md, ARCHITECTURE.md

## Canonical summary
Hustle exists to help individuals convert local skills, social trust, and visible work into economic opportunity. The platform aims to make discovery, negotiation, and payment feel natural inside a single lived experience.

## Mission themes
- Make capability visible and economically useful.
- Move from passive discovery to useful action without breaking social context.
- Give users a path from casual engagement to professional or entrepreneurial identity.

## Current implementation evidence
- The platform includes onboarding, profile elevation, chat-based negotiation, booking, escrow, and reputation features.
- The app architecture explicitly models a loop: discovery -> intent -> trust -> action -> transaction -> outcome.

## Unresolved areas
- The repository does not yet evidence a fully defined commercial or operating mission beyond the product vision and ecosystem blueprint.
