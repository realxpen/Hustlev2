# Target Users

Status: Current
Confidence: Medium
Source: PRODUCT_BLUEPRINT.md

## Canonical summary
The primary user model is a dual-role ecosystem: clients seeking local services and hustlers offering services or creative work. The experience is designed to allow users to move between these roles as their identity evolves.

## Primary segments
- Clients seeking nearby services or creative help.
- Hustlers who want to make their work discoverable and monetizable.
- People who are both consumers and creators in the same local economy.

## Evidence
- The product blueprint describes a client-to-hustler transition flow.
- The UI includes profile modes, service discovery, and booking flows consistent with these segments.

## Unresolved
- Personas are described conceptually, but the repository does not yet include a fully validated user segmentation framework.
