# Product Opportunity

Status: Current
Confidence: Medium
Source: PRODUCT_BLUEPRINT.md, ARCHITECTURE.md

## Canonical summary
The opportunity is to build a local, trust-based economic operating layer for urban and neighborhood service economies. Hustle is positioned as a place where content discovery, identity, commerce, and reinforcement all happen in the same system.

## Opportunity dimensions
- Social discovery as a gateway to commerce.
- Local relevance over broad marketplace abstraction.
- Trust signals that are visible and action-oriented.
- Identity evolution from casual consumer to verified provider.

## Implementation evidence
- The UI contains feed, discovery, profiles, chat, and transactions in one experience.
- The backend exposes service modules for booking, wallet, escrow, trust, verification, learning, and referrals.

## Open question
- The repository does not yet provide a validated business case, target market size, or go-to-market model beyond the product vision and architecture.
